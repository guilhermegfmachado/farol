Resource 08

Workshop

90-minute session, Languages Department

Objective

  • Equip colleagues for the responsible integration of AI in foreign-language teaching, producing materials adaptable to multiple learning profiles.1
  • It is not a presentation — it is a co-construction. Each participant leaves with at least one resource ready to test.1

Proposed structure (90 min)

  • 0–10 min: Framing — the problem of the mixed-needs classroom and why more of the same does not work.
  • 10–25 min: Map of the profiles — presenting Farol's six cards, with real (anonymised) cases.
  • 25–45 min: Practical demonstration — three tools, three use cases, three distinct profiles.
  • 45–75 min: Pair work — each pair adapts an existing material for a specific profile.
  • 75–85 min: Quick share — each pair shows its product in 1 minute.
  • 85–90 min: Commitments — each participant chooses a material to test, and a feedback session is set for 30 days later.

Materials needed

  • Internet access and institutional accounts for the tools demonstrated.
  • Each participant's existing materials (a worksheet, a test, or a lesson plan).
  • Printed copies of the Farol profile cards for reference.

Success indicators

  • Each participant produces at least one adapted material during the session.
  • A 30-day feedback session with at least 60% of participants reporting effective implementation.1,2
  • At least one material produced in the session is adopted as a shared department resource.
Content in Portuguese, English translation in progress.
