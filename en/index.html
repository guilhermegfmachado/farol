Tools and Approaches to Recognise and Guide students, a pocket manual for the classroom that respects the learner in front of you.
About

This compendium brings together practical strategies for teachers working with neuro-divergent, gifted, or non-native language learners. Each profile has a quick-reference card.

Each card gives three things: what you're seeing in class, what you can do today, and where AI fits in, as a tool, not a substitute. Five minutes is enough for a useful response.
