FAROL
Tools and Approaches to Recognise and Guide students, a pocket manual for the classroom that respects the learner in front of you.
About

This compendium brings together practical strategies for teachers working with neurodivergent, gifted, or non-native language learners. Each profile has a quick-reference card.

Learn more →
Start from what you see

Pick whatever is closest to what you notice in class.

Attention, pace and behaviour
Can't sit still, interrupts, distracted by everything → Forgets instructions, loses materials, doesn't finish tasks → Finishes everything fast, gets bored and disruptive → Clearly able, but underperforming →
Reading, writing and organisation
Avoids reading aloud, reads slowly and with effort → Reverses letters, writes far below how they speak → Clumsy, messy handwriting, very disorganised → Gets lost in tasks with steps and sequences → Counts on their fingers when classmates already calculate from memory → Reasons well, but mixes up numbers and forgets times tables →
Communication and language
Distressed by noise or changes in routine → Very literal, misses what's left unsaid → Just arrived, doesn't yet speak the language of instruction → Understands more than they can say in the school language →
