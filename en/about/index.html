How to use Farol

What it is, what it isn't, and how to get the most out of it in five minutes.

What it is

  • A quick-reference manual for teachers working with mixed-needs classrooms. Each card covers a learner profile and answers three questions: what you're seeing in class, what you can do today, and where AI might help.
  • Designed to be useful in five minutes, before a difficult lesson, before a meeting with parents, or when a colleague asks for a second look at a student.
  • An open resource, updated as the author's knowledge evolves. The current version is v0.1.

What it isn't

  • Not a diagnostic tool. No card replaces a formal psychological or psycho-pedagogical assessment.
  • Not a clinical manual. The language is deliberately accessible and classroom-oriented, not clinical.
  • Not definitive. Profiles are entry points, not labels. A real student rarely fits a single category.

About the AI sections

  • The Where AI fits in sections are intentionally blank in this version. The author is attending specialist training in Florence (August 2026) before recommending specific tools.
  • The structure is already in place, each tool will have a use case, a prompt suggestion, and a privacy note. Content arrives in v0.2, expected September 2026.

A note on translation

  • The full card content is translated into English. Portuguese is the original version of the guide; if anything is unclear, the Portuguese text prevails.
  • The site is designed for teachers in Portuguese-speaking contexts; the English version is provided for international colleagues and conference use.
