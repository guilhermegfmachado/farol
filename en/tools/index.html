Particularly useful for gifted students who advance beyond the curriculum, and for EAL learners with content available in multiple languages. Free version available. view tool sheet → MagicSchool AI AI platform designed specifically for teachers. Includes tools to differentiate lesson plans, create accommodations for students with SEN, generate communications for parents and guardians, and adapt materials to different reading levels. Free version available. view tool sheet → Microsoft Immersive Reader Free immersive reader integrated into Word, Teams, OneNote, and Edge. Free version available. view tool sheet → Natural Reader Converts written text to synthesised speech. Useful for students with dyslexia or dyspraxia who benefit from audio support while reading. Free version available. view tool sheet → NotebookLM Google tool that ingests documents and allows users to ask questions, generate summaries, and create study guides from the uploaded material. Useful for students with ADHD or auditory processing difficulties to follow spoken explanations, and for teachers creating lesson transcripts students can review. Effective for consolidating vocabulary and concepts, especially for profiles with limited working memory.
